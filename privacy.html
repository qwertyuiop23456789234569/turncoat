TURNCOAT - Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 October 2026

App: TURNCOAT (Android package com.tinegastudios.turncoat, and the TURNCOAT web version)

Data controller: Patrice Tinega, trading as Tinega Studios, Nairobi, Kenya

Contact: patricetinega1@gmail.com

Policy URL: https://turncoat.site/privacy.html

Summary

TURNCOAT does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal data. The game works fully offline.

What the game stores

The game saves your progress (coins, unlocked heroes, upgrades, missions, best score, route stops reached and settings such as sound and graphics level) in a file on your own device. This file never leaves your device. Tinega Studios cannot see or access it. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

What the game does not do

  • It has no user accounts and asks for no name, email, phone number or location.
  • It requests no Android permissions (no internet, location, contacts, camera, microphone or storage permission).
  • It contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • It makes no network requests while you play.

The web version is a static website. The web host that serves the game files may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) under its own privacy policy. Tinega Studios does not add any tracking to the web version.

Children

TURNCOAT is suitable for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children under 13.

Your rights

Because no personal data is collected, there is nothing to access, correct or delete on our side. The Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019 and other applicable laws still give you rights. You can contact the data controller at patricetinega1@gmail.com with any question.

Future changes

If a future version adds features that use personal data, such as ads, in-app purchases, online leaderboards or analytics, this policy will be updated before that version is released. Where the law requires it, the app will ask for your consent in the game.
